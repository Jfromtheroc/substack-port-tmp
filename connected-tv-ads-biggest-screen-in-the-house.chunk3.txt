data:text/html,<p>CTV creates demand. Search captures it. The household that saw your spot on Tuesday Googles you on Thursday, and if your search campaign is there to catch them, the loop closes. Run separately, each channel is guessing about the other’s contribution. Run together, they are a system: the big screen plants the idea, the search box harvests it. This is the simplest multi-channel play in advertising, and most businesses still run the two in isolation.</p><h2>Mistakes to avoid</h2><p>Thinking in thirty-second broadcast blocks in a world where the first five seconds decide everything. Forgetting the call to action because “it’s a branding play”: the viewer has a phone in their hand, so give them a reason to pick it up. And buying audiences too broad for the message: household targeting is precise, so use it. A spot for everyone is a spot for no one, even on the biggest screen.</p><h2>Getting started</h2>
