data:text/html,<p>They are siblings, not twins. Geofencing draws boundaries around <em>your</em> world: your trade area, your events, the places your customers gather. It is defense and discovery. Conquesting draws boundaries around <em>theirs</em>: the rival’s locations, and it is pure offense. The strongest local campaigns run both. Geofencing holds your home turf and catches demand where it forms. Conquesting goes and takes the demand that formed somewhere else. One without the other is half a strategy.</p><h2>Mistakes that waste the click</h2><p>The most common is the landing page. A conquesting click sent to a generic homepage dies there, because the visitor arrived with a comparison in mind and found no comparison. Send them somewhere that continues the argument: the offer, the price difference, the reviews, the map. The second mistake is targeting too wide: fencing every competitor in the state when your business serves one county. Relevance is the weapon. Dilute it and you are just buying impressions.</p><h2>Starting small</h2>
