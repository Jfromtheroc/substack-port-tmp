Mid campaign, it is contrast: conquest the opponent’s events and reach their attendees while the comparison is fresh. Late, it is turnout: fence the early voting sites and remind your voters, daily, that the window is open. The same tool, three phases, three messages.</p><h2>Three plays from the field</h2><p><strong>Geofence the moments.</strong> Draw a boundary around a rally, a town hall, a county fair, an early voting site, and reach the phones inside it. The most politically engaged people in the district, identified by where they chose to be.</p><p><strong>Conquest the opponent’s events.</strong> When the rival holds a rally, their attendees’ location history becomes your audience. Your message reaches them while the event is fresh.</p><p><strong>Measure the ground game.</strong> Footfall attribution reports whether the people you reached later showed up: at your events, your offices, the polls.
